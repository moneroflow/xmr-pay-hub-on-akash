MoneroFlow is a self-custody point-of-sale and payment terminal for Monero (XMR) and TRON USDT.

Generate payment links and QR codes for physical or online items, share them with customers, and confirm payments in real time. Payments go straight to your own wallet — MoneroFlow never holds your keys.

Key features:
• Send, receive and scan Monero and USDT payments
• POS-style payment links with QR codes for websites and physical stores
• Live on-chain balance with FAST and FULL sync
• Automatic node failover keeps syncs reliable
• Privacy first: all data stays on your device

MoneroFlow is free and open source under the MIT license. Your keys, your coins.
